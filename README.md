# My Favorite Series: Dragon Ball

> *"Power comes in response to a need, not a desire."* – Son Goku

## Overview
**Dragon Ball** is an iconic anime series created by Akira Toriyama. It follows **Son Goku** as he trains in martial arts, searches for the seven Dragon Balls, and protects Earth from powerful enemies.

## Main Characters
* **Son Goku** – The main hero who loves training and fighting strong opponents.
* **Vegeta** – The proud Saiyan prince and Goku's greatest rival.
* **Bulma** – A genius inventor who created the Dragon Radar.

## Why I Love It
1. **Epic Battles:** Fast-paced fights and iconic moves like the *Kamehameha*.
2. **Transformations:** Unlocking powerful forms like **Super Saiyan**.
3. **Great Story:** Inspiring themes of hard work, friendship, and never giving up.
